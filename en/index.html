Holiday apartment in Ötigheim – your second home near Rastatt

Enjoy comfort and a relaxing stay in this cosy 60 m² apartment on the edge of Ötigheim, near Rastatt in Baden-Württemberg, Germany. It accommodates up to four guests (a maximum of three adults) and is suited to holidays, business trips and longer work stays.

  • Private entrance and on-site parking

A well-equipped holiday apartment

Combined living and dining area

The living room has a pull-out sofa bed, 1.40 m wide, and plenty of space to relax. A Smart TV with IPTV and pre-installed streaming apps is also available.

The dining area has a spacious bench and chairs, with room to enjoy meals together.

Modern, compact kitchen

The kitchen includes a Senseo coffee machine, kettle, toaster, ceramic hob and oven.

Cutlery, crockery, pots, pans, spices, coffee, oil and vinegar are provided as a basic supply.

Spacious bedroom

Enjoy a restful night in a comfortable double bed with two mattresses. The room also has bedside tables and lamps, a wardrobe, a chest of drawers, and a small seating area with a table.

New, high-quality bathroom

A modern, high-quality bathroom completes your stay. It has a spacious rainfall shower and underfloor heating. A washing machine with detergent and a tumble dryer are also available.

Why stay here?

Peaceful, cool nights

The apartment is on the pleasantly cool lower-ground floor of a detached house, in a quiet cul-de-sac on the edge of town.

Convenient location

Supermarkets, bakeries and a train stop are nearby. The location is a good base for the Volksschauspiele theatre and trips to Rastatt, Karlsruhe, Baden-Baden, the Black Forest and Alsace.

Easy parking

There is a parking space on the property, with additional free parking along the street.
